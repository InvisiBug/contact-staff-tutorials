Basically and take it off with your hand. Then you can let it wrap your hand once and pop it back into the matrix. The hand wrap is to get the center point in a better position for the next finalisation of the matrix.

The other side requires a lot of sliding and is a bit harder. I haven't really practiced it as such so it's not in this video.
