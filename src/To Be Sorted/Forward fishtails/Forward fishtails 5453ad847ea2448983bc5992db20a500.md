Fishtails are one beat figures of eight on the back of the hand. Lots of tutorials have been written about them. But what I would suggest, as in the video, is to practise them one half at a time, a lot, then link them up.

Forwards is harder than reverse, no matter what people say. (They learned forwards first) Typically for the forwards fishtail there is one transition that is super hard and makes your hand curl around. Practise more. And make it look nicer than I do.
